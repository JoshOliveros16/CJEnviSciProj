Hagonoy GroundWatchWhy the ground under Hagonoy is slowly sinking

Hagonoy is a low-lying coastal town beside northern Manila Bay. Heavy groundwater pumping and soft delta soil are lowering the land by several centimeters every year, so floods get deeper and last longer.

Layer 1 Topsoil

What is land subsidence?

The ground surface slowly sinks, and the water comes in.

1

Wells pump water out

Homes, farms, fishponds and industries pull groundwater from the aquifer.

2

Clay layers compact

With less water pressure holding them up, the soft layers squeeze together.

3

The surface drops

The same tide or rain now reaches higher and stays longer.

Layer 3 Clay

Why Hagonoy?

Low coastal delta

Built from river sediments beside northern Manila Bay, about 50 km north of Manila.

Soft, squeezable ground

Delta sediments settle on their own, and faster when water is pumped out.

Heavy pumping

About 9 million m³ of groundwater is estimated to be extracted every year.

A growing town

More buildings and more people mean more weight on the ground and more water demand.

Layer 5 Aquifer

What can be done

Community

Save water, report unregistered wells and keep drains and canals clear.

Government

Limit pumping, monitor ground movement and plan flood control together.

Sustainable practices

Use other water sources, restore mangroves and let rain soak into the ground.
