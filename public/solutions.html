Mitigation and solutions

Geologist Mahar Lagmay has said subsidence could be reversible with effective policies on the over-digging of wells. No single group can do it alone.

Community actions

  • Save water at home. Fix leaks, reuse water and collect rain for non-drinking uses to cut demand on wells.
  • Report unregistered deep wells to the barangay or LGU. Groundwater use generally needs a permit.
  • Keep waterways clear. Join clean-ups of canals, creeks and drains, because garbage makes flooding worse.
  • Plan for floods. Know your evacuation route, raise appliances and documents, and follow local advisories.
  • Document and report. Photos and dates of floods, cracks and sinking help planners. Use the report form on this site.

Government initiatives

  • Groundwater limits. Extraction has been banned in parts of Bulacan since 2004, and the NWRB is tasked to enforce water-permit rules. AFP; Philippine Star
  • Monitoring. NAMRIA provided 21 benchmarks in Hagonoy and Calumpit, and GNSS stations, including one in Santo Niño, Hagonoy, track vertical movement. ISPRS, 2026
  • North Manila Bay Flood Protection Strategy. A Netherlands-funded effort that, with the Provincial Government of Bulacan, is exploring nature-based solutions. PGB
  • Master planning. Hagonoy's engineer asks for a flood and water management plan with Paombong, Malolos and Calumpit. GMA
  • Land-use planning. Researchers recommend building subsidence risk into zoning and local plans. Maclang et al.

Sustainable practices

  • Other water sources. Shift supply from deep wells to treated surface water or piped systems to ease pumping.
  • Nature-based defenses. Restore mangroves and coastal vegetation to slow waves and trap sediment. Inquirer; PGB
  • Watershed care. Reforestation and watershed conservation reduce runoff and silt problems. Inquirer, 2023
  • Let water soak in. Use permeable surfaces, retention areas and clean drains instead of sealing every surface with concrete.
  • Measure and share. School and community monitoring keep the issue visible.

Did you know? Raising roads and floors only treats the symptom. The long-term fix is to slow the sinking itself by managing how much groundwater we take.

Five things you can do this week

  1. Check for leaks

    A dripping tap or pipe wastes a surprising amount of water.

  2. Ask about wells

    Ask your barangay if nearby deep wells are registered.

  3. Clear a drain

    Remove garbage from the canal or drain closest to your home.

  4. Know your route

    Learn the evacuation route and where your family would meet.

  5. Report what you see

    Send a flood or damage report through this website.
