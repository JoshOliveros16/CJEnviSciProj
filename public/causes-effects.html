Causes and effects

What pushes the land down in Hagonoy, and what that does to the town.

Causes

Groundwater extraction

Underground water helps hold up the soil grains around it. When wells pump water out faster than nature refills it, the pressure drops and the layers above, especially soft clay, squeeze together like a sponge. The ground surface follows.

~70 mm/yraverage sinking within 500 m of pumping stations
~65 mm/yraverage farther away, so slightly slower but still sinking

Hagonoy and Calumpit, 2015 to 2021 Q1 (70.22 versus 65.31 mm/yr). Built-up volume and groundwater withdrawal together explained most of the sinking pattern (R² of about 0.85). Maclang et al., 2026

How pumping makes the land sink

  1. Wells pump

    Homes, farms, fishponds and industries draw water from deep wells.

  2. Pressure drops

    With less water between the grains, the aquifer can no longer hold up the layers above.

  3. Clay compacts

    Soft clay and silt squeeze together. This is largely permanent.

  4. Land sinks

    The surface lowers, so the same tide or rain reaches higher and lasts longer.

Other things that add to it

Urban growth and weight

More buildings, roads and fishpond dikes put more load on soft ground and raise water demand.

Natural compaction

Delta sediments slowly settle under their own weight. Pumping speeds this up.

Missing river silt

Dikes keep silt from spreading over the plain, so the land cannot rebuild the height it loses. Philippine Star, 2006

Climate change

Rising seas and stronger storms meet land that is already sinking. Sulapas et al., 2024

Effects

When the ground drops, the damage shows up where people live: flooded streets, cracked structures and salty water where there used to be dry land.

Flooding

Hagonoy floods from high tides, monsoon rain and river overflow. Subsidence makes each flood worse by lowering the land it spills onto.

  • In July 2023, 12 villages waded in nearly 5 ft (4.86 ft) of tide-driven floodwater for about a week. Manila Bulletin
  • On July 30, 2023, the whole municipality was placed under a state of calamity (Resolution 2023-87). Manila Bulletin
  • Residents have faced high-tide floods for more than a decade. Manila Bulletin

Infrastructure damage

Roads, bridges, schools, chapels and homes sit lower every year. Fixes tend to be temporary because the ground keeps moving.

  • Raising a road by about 1 m may only buy a decade at 10 cm of sinking a year. Medium feature
  • Some families raise their homes or give up flooded ground floors, and others leave. UP Resilience Institute
  • The municipal engineer said piecemeal flood projects had little effect and asked for a master plan with neighboring towns. GMA

Salty water and land loss

As the bay creeps inland, brackish water can spoil soil and groundwater, hurting farms, fishponds and drinking supplies.

  • On very gentle coastal plains, one meter of sinking can let tides reach 10 to 20 km farther inland. Philippine Star, 2006
  • At least 5,000 people have been displaced from coastal areas north of Manila as the bay advanced. AFP
